# Analysis Framework

The checklist and card structure for analyzing a stock well enough to decide on it.
FCF is the #1 KPI throughout. Every number is live-verified with a source and date.

## Data to verify per stock (live web search)

**Price & market context**
- Current price; today's move; 52-week range and where price sits in it.
- Market cap; shares outstanding (watch for recent dilution).

**Valuation**
- Trailing P/E and forward P/E (or the right multiple for the sector: EV/EBITDA,
  P/S for unprofitable growth, P/FCF, P/B for financials/miners).
- Whether the multiple is on peak or trough earnings (flag peak-on-peak cyclicals).

**Growth & profitability**
- Latest-quarter revenue growth (YoY) and the trend across recent quarters.
- Gross / operating margin and direction.

**Free cash flow — the headline KPI**
- FCF level (TTM and latest quarter), FCF margin, and trend (improving/deteriorating).
- Verdict: ✅ healthy/growing, ⚠️ cyclical/thin, or ❌ negative/burning cash.
- Any name with negative or worsening FCF gets that flagged at the top of its card.

**Balance sheet**
- Net cash vs net debt; ability to self-fund capex; recent raises or buybacks.

**Last-7-days news scan** (any of these can change a thesis)
- Earnings/guidance, analyst up/downgrades, secondary offerings, insider buying/selling,
  M&A, regulatory or political actions, major customer/partner news.

## Card fields (what every stock card contains)

1. Ticker, company name, sector/industry badge, rating chip (1–10 + BUY/HOLD/SELL).
2. Current price (with date) and 52-week context.
3. **What it does** — one plain sentence.
4. **Best at** — one sentence on its edge.
5. **Works with** — named partners/customers.
6. **Differentiation / moat** — why it wins vs peers; how durable.
7. **Key KPIs** — market cap, trailing/fwd P/E, rev growth, and **FCF with ✅/⚠️/❌**.
8. **Bull thesis** — 1–2 sentences, confirmed against the data.
9. **Bear case / failure mode** — the strongest argument against, stated honestly.
10. **Catalysts** — upcoming dated events (earnings date, product, regulatory).
11. **12-month forecast** — bear / base / bull with brief reasoning (labeled estimates).
12. **Verdict + tripwire** — the rating justification and the one thing that would flip it.

## Comparison & portfolio mode

- When comparing names, add a sortable scoreboard (rating, FCF verdict, fwd P/E,
  rev growth) and call out the best risk-adjusted pick.
- For a portfolio with dollar allocations: show an allocation donut, confirm allocations
  sum to the stated total, map each position to its failure mode, and compute concentration
  (e.g. "X% is really one bet on theme Y"). Stress-test the book against 2–3 scenarios with
  rough P&L.

## Optional: multi-agent deep dive (for high-stakes / real-money decisions)

When the user is deploying real capital or explicitly wants a committee review, run the
named positions through five independent lenses, then synthesize:

1. **Equity Research** — verify every KPI; confirm or refute each thesis; flag 7-day news.
2. **Risk Manager** — exposure overlaps, failure modes, scenario stress tests, sizing vs conviction.
3. **Macro Strategist** — rates/CPI/sector backdrop; does macro favor or fight each sleeve.
4. **Devil's Advocate** — attack the book; steelman the rejected names; argue underperformance.
5. **Execution & Tax** — earnings-date conflicts, deployment calendar, wash-sale/tax flags.

**Synthesis rules:** default is CONFIRM; a change must be supported by ≥2 independent lenses
AND survive the devil's advocate. Show disagreements explicitly rather than averaging them.
End with a scorecard (CONFIRM/RESIZE/REPLACE/SELL), a revised table, top-3 missed risks, a
bear/base/bull return estimate, and a "what would make us wrong" tripwire checklist.

## Sourcing notes

- Good live sources: company filings/IR, stockanalysis.com, exchange data, reputable
  financial news, and sector-specific trackers. Cite source + date for each figure.
- For non-USD names, present financials in the native currency (don't fake an FX conversion);
  label axes and per-share figures in that currency.
